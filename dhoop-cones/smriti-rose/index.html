Smriti — Rose Dhoop Cones

Smriti is the Sanskrit word for memory and remembrance. Inspired by the fragrance of roses and the memories that make a place feel like home, these dhoop cones bring a gentle floral warmth to your everyday rituals.

  • A soft, warm and gently sweet rose fragrance.
  • Ideal for daily puja, evening rituals and creating a welcoming atmosphere.
  • A fragrant addition to moments spent reconnecting with familiar traditions.
  • Each pack contains 28 dhoop cones.
  • Designed for use with a suitable, heat-resistant dhoop cone holder.

How to Use

  1. 1. Position the cone: Place one dhoop cone upright on a stable, heat-resistant holder.
  2. 2. Light the tip: Use a match or lighter to ignite the pointed tip until a flame appears.
  3. 3. Extinguish the flame: Gently blow out the flame and allow the cone to smoulder.
  4. 4. Enjoy the fragrance: Let the soft aroma of roses fill your space. Never leave a burning cone unattended.

Bring Smriti home and let your favourite moments live on through fragrance.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
