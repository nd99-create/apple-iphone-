Dhoop Cones

Dhoop cones bring a concentrated fragrance to everyday puja and home rituals. Explore five classic Indian fragrances: rose, mogra, sandalwood, lavender and jasmine. Each pack contains 28 dhoop cones and costs ₹200, with free delivery across India. Choose rose dhoop for a soft floral note, mogra for a rich blossom fragrance, sandalwood for warm woods, lavender for a herbal character, or jasmine for a bright floral feel. These incense cones are designed for use with a suitable, heat-resistant cone holder. Whether you call them dhoop batti or puja dhoop, choose a fragrance that belongs in your daily moments. Browse each scent below and use the cones according to the product instructions. Keep the holder on a stable, heat-resistant surface while a cone is burning.
