Kusuma is a Sanskrit word meaning flower or blossom. Inspired by the timeless charm of mogra, these dhoop cones celebrate the floral traditions of Indian homes, bringing a familiar fragrance to your prayers, festive preparations and everyday moments.

  • A creamy, rich and floral mogra fragrance.
  • Ideal for morning puja, festive rituals and everyday devotional practices.
  • A thoughtful way to bring the fragrance of blossoms into your home.
  • Each pack contains 28 dhoop cones.
  • Designed for use with a suitable, heat-resistant dhoop cone holder.

How to Use

  1. 1. Position the cone: Place one dhoop cone upright on a stable, heat-resistant holder.
  2. 2. Light the tip: Use a match or lighter to ignite the pointed tip until a flame appears.
  3. 3. Extinguish the flame: Gently blow out the flame and allow the cone to smoulder.
  4. 4. Enjoy the fragrance: Let the floral aroma of mogra accompany your everyday rituals. Never leave a burning cone unattended.

Bring Kusuma home and let the beauty of blossoms become part of your daily rituals.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
