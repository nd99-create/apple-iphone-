Dhara means a stream, flow or continuous course. Inspired by the grounding character of sandalwood, these dhoop cones invite you to slow down and reconnect with the rituals that bring meaning to everyday life.

  • A smooth, creamy and woody sandalwood fragrance.
  • Suited to prayer, meditation and traditional home rituals.
  • A warm aromatic addition to moments of reflection and quiet.
  • Each pack contains 28 dhoop cones.
  • Designed for use with a suitable, heat-resistant dhoop cone holder.

How to Use

  1. 1. Position the cone: Place one dhoop cone upright on a stable, heat-resistant holder.
  2. 2. Light the tip: Use a match or lighter to ignite the pointed tip until a flame appears.
  3. 3. Extinguish the flame: Gently blow out the flame and allow the cone to smoulder.
  4. 4. Enjoy the fragrance: Let the woody aroma of sandalwood accompany your moments of prayer and reflection. Never leave a burning cone unattended.

Bring Dhara home and let your daily rituals flow at their own pace.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
