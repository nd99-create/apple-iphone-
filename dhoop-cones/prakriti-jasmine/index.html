Prakriti means nature and the natural world. Inspired by the bright floral character of jasmine, these dhoop cones bring a touch of nature-inspired fragrance to your home, from morning rituals to welcoming guests.

  • A bright, green and sunny jasmine fragrance.
  • Ideal for morning puja, festive preparations and everyday home rituals.
  • A floral addition to spaces where you gather, reflect and reconnect.
  • Each pack contains 28 dhoop cones.
  • Designed for use with a suitable, heat-resistant dhoop cone holder.

How to Use

  1. 1. Position the cone: Place one dhoop cone upright on a stable, heat-resistant holder.
  2. 2. Light the tip: Use a match or lighter to ignite the pointed tip until a flame appears.
  3. 3. Extinguish the flame: Gently blow out the flame and allow the cone to smoulder.
  4. 4. Enjoy the fragrance: Let the bright floral aroma of jasmine bring a nature-inspired touch to your home. Never leave a burning cone unattended.
