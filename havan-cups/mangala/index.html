Mangala represents auspiciousness, good fortune and blessed beginnings. Inspired by the significance of sacred fire in Indian traditions, these havan cups are intended for meaningful occasions, devotional practices and moments that bring families together.

  • Inspired by traditional havan and sacred fire rituals.
  • Suitable for appropriate devotional practices and auspicious occasions, following the product's instructions.
  • A thoughtful addition to festive preparations, housewarmings and traditional ceremonies.

How to Use

  1. 1. Prepare the space: Place the havan cup on a stable, non-flammable, heat-resistant surface in a well-ventilated area.
  2. 2. Light the cup: Ignite the designated point according to the instructions supplied with the product.
  3. 3. Allow it to burn safely: Let the cup burn as directed. Keep it away from curtains, flammable materials, children and pets, and never leave it unattended.
  4. 4. Allow it to cool: Wait until the cup and its contents have cooled completely before handling or disposing of the remains.
