## Design Context

### Users
Indian customers shopping for Ruhani Room incense on mobile. They need a straightforward way to choose products, manage a cart, enter delivery details, and pay.

### Brand Personality
Bright, friendly, playful. The experience should still feel trustworthy and calm when asking for personal or payment details.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first shopping within the existing Ruhani Room visual identity: warm ivory, smoke, sand, and bronze, with clear, friendly checkout steps and no abrupt visual shift.

### Design Principles
- Keep product selection and checkout comfortable on a phone.
- Make quantities, delivery details, and the full payable amount easy to understand before payment.
- Use friendly, reassuring language and clear feedback at every step.
- Never collect or expose Razorpay secrets in browser code.
