## Design Context

### Users
Indian customers discovering Ruhani Room, a premium home-fragrance and ritual brand, primarily on mobile. They want to explore scents, choose individual products or festive bundles, and move confidently from basket to delivery and secure payment.

### Brand Personality
Sun-warmed, ceremonial, tactile. The experience should feel welcoming and richly Indian, while staying clear, calm and trustworthy when asking for personal or payment details.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial, vintage-haveli atmosphere: maroon, warm ivory, sand and brass, with late-afternoon sunlight, tactile imagery and measured motion. Keep the palette and typography cohesive from the homepage through payment.

### Design Principles
- Let product photography, fragrance cues and generous spacing carry the story.
- Make product selection, bundles and checkout comfortable on a phone.
- Make quantities, delivery details, and the full payable amount easy to understand before payment.
- Use warm, direct language and clear feedback at every step.
- Never collect or expose Razorpay secrets in browser code.
