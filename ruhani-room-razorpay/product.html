Burning and care

  • Burn in a heat-safe holder on a stable, non-flammable surface.
  • Keep the room lightly ventilated while incense is burning.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains and paper.
  • Never leave burning incense unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
