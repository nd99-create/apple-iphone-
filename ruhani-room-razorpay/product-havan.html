Havan Cups

A small havan, ready to light

Each cup comes filled with a blend of herbs and seeds, so you can perform a simple havan at home without measuring out loose samagri. Place a cup in a heat-safe dish, light the blend and let the fragrant smoke fill the room.

Use them for daily puja, festivals, griha pravesh, or any moment you want to mark with gratitude.

Six cups per pack. Free shipping.

Burning and care

  • Always place the cup in a metal or ceramic dish before lighting.
  • Light in a ventilated room, away from curtains and paper.
  • Keep away from children and pets, and never leave it unattended.
  • Let the dish cool fully before clearing it.
