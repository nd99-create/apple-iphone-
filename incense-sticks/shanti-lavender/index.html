Shanti — Lavender Incense Sticks

Shanti is the Sanskrit word for peace and tranquillity. Inspired by the desire for slower, quieter moments, these lavender incense sticks bring a gentle aromatic touch to your evening routine and your favourite corners of home.

  • A clean, herbal and calming lavender fragrance.
  • Ideal for winding down in the evening, reading or enjoying a quiet moment to yourself.
  • A lovely companion to mindful routines and peaceful home rituals.
  • Each pack contains 28 incense sticks.
  • Designed for use with a suitable incense stick holder.

How to Use

  1. Hold a lit match or lighter to the tip of the incense stick until it catches fire.
  2. Allow the tip to burn briefly, then gently blow out the flame.
  3. Place the stick securely in a heat-resistant incense holder.
  4. Enjoy the gentle herbal fragrance as the incense smoulders.

Bring Shanti home and make room for a little more stillness in your day.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
