Named after Mallika, a traditional Sanskrit name associated with jasmine, this fragrance celebrates the delicate beauty of mogra blossoms. Floral and comforting, it brings to mind fresh flowers, peaceful mornings and the timeless rituals of Indian homes.

  • A creamy, rich and floral mogra fragrance.
  • Perfect for morning prayers, festive preparations and everyday home rituals.
  • Creates a welcoming atmosphere for moments of stillness and reflection.
  • Each pack contains 28 incense sticks.
  • Designed for use with a suitable incense stick holder.

How to Use

  1. Hold a lit match or lighter to the tip of the incense stick until it catches fire.
  2. Allow the tip to burn briefly, then gently blow out the flame.
  3. Place the stick securely in a heat-resistant incense holder.
  4. Enjoy the delicate floral fragrance as it fills your space.

Bring Mallika home and let the beauty of flowers linger a little longer.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
