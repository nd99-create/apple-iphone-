Chandana means sandalwood, a fragrance deeply associated with Indian traditions and sacred rituals. With its warm, woody character, this incense is made for moments when you want to slow down, reconnect with yourself and bring a sense of familiarity to your surroundings.

  • A smooth, creamy and woody sandalwood fragrance.
  • Well suited to meditation, prayer and quiet evening rituals.
  • A comforting fragrance for creating a warm and inviting atmosphere at home.
  • Each pack contains 28 incense sticks.
  • Designed for use with a suitable incense stick holder.

How to Use

  1. Hold a lit match or lighter to the tip of the incense stick until it catches fire.
  2. Allow the tip to burn briefly, then gently blow out the flame.
  3. Place the stick securely in a heat-resistant incense holder.
  4. Enjoy the warm, woody fragrance as the incense smoulders.

Bring Chandana home and rediscover the quiet beauty of timeless traditions.

Never leave burning incense unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable materials.
